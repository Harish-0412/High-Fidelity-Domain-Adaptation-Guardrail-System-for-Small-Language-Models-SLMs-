Claim review guidance:

CPT 99214 may be reported for an established patient office visit when the
documentation supports the required level of medical decision making or the
applicable time threshold. Billing staff should verify that the record includes
the presenting problem, clinically relevant assessment, plan, and risk level.

When procedure codes are billed with an evaluation and management service on
the same date, the record must support that the E/M service is distinct from
the work normally included in the procedure. Missing documentation should be
flagged for review before claim submission.
