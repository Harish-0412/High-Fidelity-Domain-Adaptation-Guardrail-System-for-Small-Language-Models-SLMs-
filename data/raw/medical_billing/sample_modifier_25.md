# Medical Billing Sample: Modifier 25

Modifier 25 is used to indicate that a significant, separately identifiable
evaluation and management service was performed by the same physician or
qualified health care professional on the same day as another procedure or
service.

The documentation should show that the evaluation and management service was
above and beyond the usual preoperative or postoperative work associated with
the procedure. The note should clearly support the separate complaint,
assessment, medical decision making, or time when time-based coding is used.

Modifier 25 should not be appended merely because a procedure and an office
visit occurred on the same date. If the evaluation and management work is only
the usual work included in the procedure, separate billing may not be supported.
